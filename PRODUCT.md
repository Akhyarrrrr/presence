# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Organization owners, attendance administrators, and members checking in through a managed station.

## Product Purpose

Provide self-service, tenant-isolated attendance operations with reliable fallback, correction, and audit workflows.

## Brand Personality

Operational, reassuring, accountable.

## Anti-references

Biometric overclaims, data that is collected then discarded, decorative admin UI, and attendance dead ends.

## Design Principles

- Never trap a member behind biometric failure.
- Preserve an auditable reason for every exception and correction.
- Keep each organization strictly isolated.
- Optimize recurring admin work for batches rather than repetition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, non-biometric attendance alternatives, keyboard-complete administration, large touch targets, and reduced motion.
